# MedStart — Vision

## Mission

Democratise access to world-class medical education by connecting students and educators on a platform built specifically for the demands of healthcare training.

## Problem

Medical education is fragmented. Students rely on outdated textbooks, disconnected forums, and geography-constrained tutoring. Educators lack tools purpose-built for clinical teaching. Existing e-learning platforms are generic — they treat medicine like any other subject.

## Our answer

MedStart is a vertical SaaS platform for medical education. It is not an LMS adapted for medicine. It is designed from the ground up for the way clinicians teach and students learn:

- **Structured curricula** aligned with international standards (GMC, USMLE, PLAB, etc.)
- **Verified educators** — every profile manually reviewed
- **Adaptive learning paths** based on individual gaps and goals
- **Real-time session tools** — annotated imaging, OSCE simulation, case studies
- **Progress analytics** that inform both student and educator

## Who we serve

### Primary users

| Persona | Description |
|---|---|
| **Medical student** | Years 1–6, preparing for exams (USMLE Step 1/2, PLAB, finals), needs structured guidance |
| **Foundation doctor** | Post-graduation, seeking specialty preparation, clinical skills revision |
| **Medical educator** | Consultant, registrar, or academic who wants to teach, earn supplementary income, and give back |

### Secondary users

| Persona | Description |
|---|---|
| **Medical school** | Institutional accounts for faculty-led supplementary teaching |
| **Admin** | Internal MedStart team managing platform integrity |

## Principles

1. **Medicine-first** — Every design decision asks "does this serve the clinical education context?"
2. **Trust is the product** — Credentials must be verifiable. Wrong medical information is dangerous.
3. **Simplicity wins** — Medical students are time-poor. Every interaction must be fast and frictionless.
4. **Educators are professionals** — Treat them as partners, not content creators on a gig platform.
5. **Evidence-based** — Platform decisions should be grounded in learning science, not convention.

## What MedStart is NOT

- Not a MOOC platform (video lectures alone)
- Not a question bank (exam prep only)
- Not a social network for doctors
- Not a job board
- Not a generic tutoring marketplace

## Success metrics (long-term)

- Student pass rates improve vs. control
- Educator NPS > 70
- Monthly active students / total enrolled > 60%
- Time-to-first-session < 48h after registration
